Transparent Workflow

How The System Works

A streamlined 3-step digital process ensuring timely resolution.

1
Submit Your Grievance

Fill out the online form with category, precise location, and upload supporting photos or documents. A unique Complaint ID is instantly assigned.

2
Department Assignment

The municipal administrator reviews the complaint, routes it to the designated department (e.g. Water Board, Electricity), and assigns field officers.

3
Field Action & Resolution

Officers rectify the issue on-site, record official resolution remarks, and update the status to RESOLVED with immediate notifications to the citizen.
